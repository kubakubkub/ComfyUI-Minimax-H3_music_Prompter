The bassline is deep and round, mixed to hug the kick. The whole track is saturated, slightly lo-fi and glued, with the drums proudly in front.

Arrangement
Instrument Lifecycle Description (Primary/Secondary Layering):
Primary: The swung boom-bap drum break -- deep kick, cracking snare, dusty hats with ghost-note swing -- runs continuously, paired with a round, walking bassline that anchors the harmony.
Secondary: A chopped soul-sample loop (filtered keys and a fragment of horn) carries the melody, re-chopped differently between verse and hook; a faint string pad thickens the hooks.
Groove & Foundation Progression: The intro plays the raw drum break with vinyl static and a filtered tease of the sample loop, which opens fully as verse one begins. The bridge flips the sample chop into a new pattern under a half-time drum feel, and the final hook rides the fullest stack before the outro strips back to drums and crackle.
Embellishments, Textures & Spatial FX: Turntable scratches and record rewinds mark section changes; vinyl crackle beds the whole track; occasional horn stabs and a filtered radio-static sweep accent transitions.
